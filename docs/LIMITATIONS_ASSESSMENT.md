# Limitations Assessment

## What Prototype 0.1 establishes beyond definitions

It establishes more than `Violation := Conflict`; no such alias exists. General
theorems connect independently stated attachment, identity, record-integrity,
and role predicates to a computed result. Concrete countermodels demonstrate
that conflict, compliance, indeterminacy, nonattachment, and displacement are
all inhabited. Cross-source countermodels show federal and New York predicates
are not interchangeable. Trace proofs show a repair can be reachable and
append-only.

These are substantive model properties, but their legal force is conditional
on the truth and sufficiency of every encoded premise.

## What it does not establish

- No real person, record, proceeding, or agency action was tested.
- No theorem is named or defined as a judicially determined constitutional
  violation.
- The project does not prove the truth of identity, role, waiver, custody,
  arrest, trigger, or record-completeness facts.
- It does not formalize the complete preliminary hearing, the final revocation
  hearing, initial parole release, administrative appeal, or Article 78 review.
- It does not determine cure, prejudice, harmless error, mootness, timeliness,
  exhaustion, standing, immunity, remedy, or relief.
- It does not predict an outcome or advise anyone to file.

## Significant legal blockers

1. Complete current citator and New York implementation review.
2. Resolve federal coverage for New York statuses other than literal parole.
3. Formalize stage triggers and displacement across detention, recognizance,
   technical/nontechnical allegations, admissions, waiver, and new conviction.
4. Define valid waiver from authoritative law and admissible evidence.
5. Define matter-scoped, time-scoped actor identity and supervisory involvement.
6. Research the legal effect of later independent process on an earlier defect.
7. Version § 259-i before the scheduled 2027-09-01 changes.

## Engineering limits

The model uses minimal synthetic traces. It has no parser, authenticated-source
layer, temporal interval type, provenance object, cryptographic evidence
binding, or integration with PPI. `Evidence.verified` is a premise, not a
verification service.

## Preprint release status

The reproducibility gate passes for a standalone research preprint because the
build, negative controls, source separation, and dependency checks are
reproducible. The legal blockers above remain unresolved, and independent legal
and formal-methods reviews have not been obtained. Public availability must not
be presented as legal approval, peer review, production suitability, or legal
reliance, and the project remains separate from PPI.
