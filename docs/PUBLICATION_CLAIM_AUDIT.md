## Purpose

This matrix is the cross-document control for:

- `publications/markdown/ONE_PAGE_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` (`Summary`);
- `publications/markdown/PUBLIC_RESEARCH_REPORT.md` (`Report`); and
- `publications/markdown/TECHNICAL_RESEARCH_PAPER.md` (`Paper`).

It identifies the authority for each major claim, the exact formal result where
one exists, necessary premises, nonvacuous examples, limitations, and external
review responsibility. A row marked “model rule” is not attributed to a legal
source. “Machine checked” means that Lean checked an implication from encoded
definitions and supplied premises; it does not authenticate those premises.

## Cross-document vocabulary control

| Term | Required meaning in all three manuscripts | Prohibited implication |
|---|---|---|
| `verified` | A value supplied to the model under the `Evidence.verified` constructor, including actor and stage values. | Lean authenticated a document, role entry, relationship, or fact. |
| `complete` | An externally supplied `RecordIntegrity.complete` label required for narrow compliance. | The project independently proved all relevant evidence was collected. |
| `validated` | An externally supplied gate permitting installation of a role-event-prefix-extending candidate. | Lean verified reviewer competence, provenance, or legal adequacy. |
| `conflict` | A source-specific model classification under explicit premises. | A court found a constitutional or statutory violation. |
| `compliant` | Narrow satisfaction of the selected actor-separation predicate. | Overall hearing, institutional, or legal compliance. |
| `indeterminate` | Supplied evidence is insufficient for this classifier. | The proceeding was lawful, unlawful, or factually unknowable. |
| `repair` | A formal evidence-workflow operation or prospective reassignment, as specifically identified. | Cure, harmlessness, mootness, prejudice resolution, or remedy. |
| `role-entry prefix preserved` | The earlier role-event list remains a prefix; specific synthetic proofs also keep original and later `Matter` values separately available. | A complete conflict/assessment history is embedded in the later matter, or every entry is authentic, correctly ordered, or complete. |

## Audit disposition rule

Any reviewer objection must receive one of four dispositions:

- `accepted`;
- `revision required`;
- `disputed interpretation`; or
- `out of scope`.

No claim marked `revision required` may remain unresolved before publication.
A disputed interpretation may remain only if the manuscripts identify it as
disputed, avoid presenting it as settled, and the release decision expressly
accepts that treatment.
