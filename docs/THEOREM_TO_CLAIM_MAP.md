# Theorem-to-Claim Map

| Theorem | Formal result | Source/claim | Dependencies and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| `federal_verified_disqualifier_conflict` | Supplied federal coverage, required stage, stable actor, usable record, and reporter/recommender role entry entail both computed `conflict` and the independent structured predicate `FederalConflictEstablished`. | `CPF-FED-001`; *Morrissey*, 408 U.S. at 485–86. | `verified` is a constructor name, not source authentication; the theorem does not prove waiver validity, source facts, remedy, prejudice, or a judicial violation finding. |
| `ny_verified_disqualifier_conflict` | Corresponding rule for the separately modeled, externally interpreted New York prior-supervisory-involvement entry. | `CPF-NY-001`; Exec. Law § 259-i(3)(c)(i). | Lean does not reconstruct the supervisory relationship; current-version statutory model conflict only, not automatically a federal constitutional conflict. |
| `federal_verified_uninvolved_compliant` | Complete role record plus no federal disqualifier entails narrow federal actor-separation compliance. | `CPF-FED-002`. | Does not establish overall hearing compliance. Completeness is an express premise. |
| `ny_verified_uninvolved_compliant` | Complete record plus no New York disqualifier entails narrow statutory actor-separation compliance. | `CPF-NY-002`. | Same narrow limit; no equivalence to federal doctrine. |
| `missing_decisionmaker_indeterminate`; `contradictory_decisionmaker_indeterminate` | Missing or contradictory identity blocks classification. | `CPF-EVID-001`. | Evidence rule is a research design choice supporting fact fidelity. |
| `verified_waiver_displaces` | An externally verified valid waiver displaces the required-stage assessment. | `CPF-STAGE-001`; § 259-i(3)(d). | Lean does not determine that a waiver is legally valid. |
| `federal_initial_applicant_not_applicable` | Literal initial-release applicant status does not activate this narrow *Morrissey* preliminary-revocation predicate. | `CPF-FED-003`; *Morrissey*, 408 U.S. at 480–87. | Other constitutional or state-law theories remain outside this theorem. |
| `prospective_reassignment_preserves_integrity`; `prospective_reassignment_preserves_role_history` | Reassignment appends actor events while retaining both the prior event history and its existing integrity classification. | `CPF-REPAIR-001`. | Reassignment alone supplies no new evidence and says nothing about legal cure. |
| `reassignment_alone_cannot_establish_completeness`; `federal_incomplete_reassignment_indeterminate` | A non-complete record remains non-complete after actor reassignment; an incomplete uninvolved record remains indeterminate. | `CPF-REPAIR-002`. | General fail-closed repair invariant. |
| `unvalidated_evidence_repair_is_noop`; `evidence_completion_requires_validated_complete_candidate` | An unvalidated submission changes nothing; conversion from non-complete to complete requires both validated status and a complete candidate. | `CPF-REPAIR-002`. | Validation is an explicit model premise, not an authentication service. |
| `federal_prospective_reassignment_compliant` | Reassignment can support narrow compliance only when the original record was already complete and the replacement is uninvolved. | `CPF-REPAIR-001`. | Does not erase or remedy historical conflict. |
| `federal_validated_evidence_repair_compliant` | A validated, complete, history-extending candidate can support narrow prospective compliance when attachment, identity, and uninvolvement are separately proved. | `CPF-REPAIR-003`. | Does not prove real-world validation or legal cure. |
| `reachable_append` | Two compatible reachable traces compose. | `CPF-TRACE-001`. | Operational theorem, not a legal rule. |
| `verified_federal_disqualifying_involvement`; `verified_federal_conflict_has_independent_structure` | Nonvacuous synthetic federal conflict. | `CPF-FED-001`. | Synthetic only. |
| `different_uninvolved_decisionmaker_complies`; `same_agency_does_not_create_federal_conflict` | Nonvacuous compliant example; same agency alone does not trigger the modeled disqualifier. | `CPF-FED-002`; *Morrissey*, 408 U.S. at 485–86. | Does not resolve other bias theories. |
| `missing_identity_is_indeterminate`; `contradictory_identity_is_indeterminate` | Negative evidence controls. | `CPF-EVID-001`. | No factual inference from a gap. |
| `initial_release_does_not_attach`; `initial_release_cannot_establish_this_revocation_conflict` | Wrong-posture negative control. | `CPF-FED-003`. | Limited to this protection. |
| `verified_waiver_displaces_preliminary_assessment`; `verified_waiver_blocks_required_stage_conflict_classification` | Displaced-stage negative control. | `CPF-STAGE-001`. | External waiver-validation blocker remains. |
| `verified_ny_prior_supervisory_involvement`; `verified_ny_conflict_has_independent_structure` | Nonvacuous synthetic New York conflict. | `CPF-NY-001`. | Synthetic, current statutory version only. |
| `federal_conflict_does_not_force_ny_conflict`; `ny_attachment_can_exceed_narrow_federal_status` | Countermodels prove the two source predicates and coverage functions do not collapse. | `CPF-SEPARATE-001`. | Model distinction, not a complete doctrinal comparison. |
| `original_conflict_remains_auditable_after_reassignment`; `original_conflict_history_survives_validated_repair` | Original conflict, later narrow compliance, and history preservation can coexist without rewriting the original matter. | `CPF-REPAIR-001`, `CPF-REPAIR-003`. | No cure/remedy conclusion. |
| `initial_conflict_trace_reachable`; `repair_trace_reachable`; `repaired_full_trace_reachable`; `repaired_trace_preserves_event_history` | Synthetic conflict and repair traces are reachable and composable without deleting history. | `CPF-TRACE-001`. | Trace semantics are intentionally minimal. |
